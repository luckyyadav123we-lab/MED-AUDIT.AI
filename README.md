# 🏥 MedAudit — AI-Powered Healthcare Intelligence

**Smarter Healthcare. Secure Records. Faster Claims.**

MedAudit is an AI-powered healthcare SaaS platform designed to simplify hospital operations through intelligent insurance claim verification, digital medical records, and secure patient data access.

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Claim Verification** — Detects missing information, documentation errors, and inconsistencies in insurance claims.
- 🧠 **Multi-Agent AI Workflow** — Uses specialized AI agents to analyze claim documents and generate structured verification reports.
- 🗂️ **Electronic Health Records (EHR)** — Centralized management of patient medical records and documents.
- 📲 **NFC & QR Integration** — Enables convenient patient identification and controlled medical record access.
- 🚑 **Emergency Health Card** — Provides authorized access to essential emergency information.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Role-Based Dashboards** — Dedicated workflows for administrators, doctors, and patients.
- 🔐 **Secure Data Access** — Designed around access controls, consent, temporary permissions, and audit logs.

## 💡 Problem We Solve

Healthcare providers often face manual paperwork, fragmented patient records, and incomplete insurance documentation. These issues can cause delays, administrative overhead, and claim rejections.

**MedAudit aims to make healthcare documentation more efficient, connected, and transparent.**

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Patients upload and manage their medical records.
2. Healthcare staff submit insurance-related documents for verification.
3. AI agents analyze documentation and identify potential issues.
4. The platform generates a structured verification report.
5. Authorized professionals review the results before final decisions.
